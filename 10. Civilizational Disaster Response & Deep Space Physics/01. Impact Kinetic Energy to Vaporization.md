**01. Impact Kinetic Energy to Vaporization: The Absolute Deflection Threshold**

**Part 1: Current State** (as of November 2026)

NASA's Double Asteroid Redirection Test (DART) demonstrated in 2022 that a kinetic impactor can measurably alter an asteroid's orbital period. The DART impactor, with a mass of approximately 580 kg and impact velocity of approximately 6.1 km/s, shortened Dimorphos's orbital period around Didymos by 33 minutes ± 1 minute—a deflection exceeding pre-mission predictions . Reconstructed ejecta momentum yields a momentum enhancement factor β_D = 2.13 ± 0.19 for Dimorphos, with system-level β_sys = 2.04 ± 0.19 . ESA's Hera spacecraft arrived at the Didymos system in late 2026 and has begun close-range characterization from 25–30 km, with detailed crater imaging planned for November 25 . This document acknowledges that progress. The physical deadlock addressed here is not deflection feasibility at the Dimorphos scale. It is the energy-density threshold above which the momentum-transfer mechanism itself ceases to exist.

❌ **Core Physical Deadlock**: Kinetic deflection relies on momentum transfer through directed ejecta. The momentum enhancement factor β arises from material ejected at speeds exceeding the asteroid's escape velocity. DART's reconstructed ejecta budget confirms β_D = 2.13 ± 0.19 for Dimorphos, meaning ejected material contributed more than the impactor's own momentum . When impact energy density at the coupling point exceeds the vaporization threshold of crustal silicates, the target material transitions from solid-fragment ejecta to vapor. Vapor expands isotropically; it does not carry directed momentum along the impact vector. The enhancement factor β collapses toward 1—the inelastic collision limit. Above this threshold, adding impactor mass or velocity does not scale deflection; it scales vaporization.

❌ **Engineering Misconception**: The assumption that β remains constant or increases with impact energy is physically invalid above the vaporization threshold. DART's β measurement is a low-energy-density regime result. The β parameter depends on target porosity, strength, surface curvature, and boulder distribution . The DART target Dimorphos is a rubble-pile body with bulk density constrained to 1435 kg/m³ (range: 1281–1548 kg/m³) . A stronger, more coherent target would produce less ejecta and lower β. A higher-energy impact would cross the vaporization threshold and collapse the ejecta mechanism entirely. Extrapolating Dimorphos-scale β to arbitrary targets or higher energies violates the phase transition physics.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (Only Exit)**: The only physical exit from the vaporization deadlock is to abandon momentum-transfer deflection above the threshold and adopt energy-deposition topologies that operate below the fragmentation limit. Standoff nuclear ablation deposits X-ray energy into a surface layer of micrometer-to-millimeter depth, producing directed blow-off without bulk vaporization of the coupling volume . Three-dimensional hydrodynamic simulations of a ~160-meter asteroid show that above a certain energy deposition threshold, approximately 97% of material exceeds escape velocity and ~98% reaches complete damage—fragmentation, not deflection . The topology requires precursor characterization of the target's material properties—porosity, strength, and equation of state—because the optimal standoff distance is a function of these parameters. Without precursor data, standoff ablation is a blind deployment: too close and the asteroid fragments; too far and the impulse is insufficient.

**01. 撞击动能至汽化相变：绝对偏转阈值**

**第一部分：现状**（截止至2026年11月）

NASA的DART任务于2022年证明，动能撞击器可 measurably 改变小行星轨道周期。DART撞击器质量约580 kg，撞击速度约6.1 km/s，将Dimorphos绕Didymos的轨道周期缩短了33分钟±1分钟——偏转量超过任务前预测。重建的抛射物动量给出Dimorphos的动量增强因子β_D = 2.13 ± 0.19，系统级β_sys = 2.04 ± 0.19 。ESA的Hera航天器于2026年末抵达Didymos系统，已开始在25–30 km距离进行近距离表征，详细撞击坑成像计划于11月25日发布。本文承认上述进展。此处指出的物理死锁不是Dimorphos尺度的偏转可行性问题。它是能量密度超过某一阈值后，动量传递机制本身不再存在的物理死锁。

❌ **核心物理死锁**：动能偏转依赖通过定向抛射物实现的动量传递。动量增强因子β来源于以超过小行星逃逸速度抛射的物质。DART的重建抛射物预算确认Dimorphos的β_D = 2.13 ± 0.19，即抛射物贡献的动量超过撞击器自身动量。当耦合点的撞击能量密度超过地壳硅酸盐的汽化阈值时，靶材从固体碎片抛射转变为蒸汽。蒸汽各向同性膨胀；它不携带沿撞击矢量方向的定向动量。增强因子β坍缩至1——非弹性碰撞极限。在该阈值之上，增加撞击器质量或速度不缩放偏转；它缩放汽化。

❌ **工程认知误区**：假设β随撞击能量保持恒定或增加，在汽化阈值之上物理上无效。DART的β测量值是低能量密度区间的结果。β参数依赖于靶材孔隙率、强度、表面曲率与巨石分布。DART目标Dimorphos是碎石堆天体，体密度约束为1435 kg/m³（区间：1281–1548 kg/m³）。更强、更完整的靶材产生更少抛射物，β更低。更高能量的撞击将穿越汽化阈值并彻底摧毁抛射机制。将Dimorphos尺度的β外推至任意靶材或更高能量违反相变物理。

✅ **系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）**：汽化死锁的唯一物理生路，是放弃阈值之上的动量传递偏转，采用在碎裂极限以下运行的能量沉积拓扑。Standoff核烧蚀将X射线能量沉积到微米至毫米深度的表层，产生定向吹离而不使耦合体积发生体汽化。对约160米小行星的三维流体动力学模拟显示，超过某一能量沉积阈值后，约97%的物质超过逃逸速度且约98%达到完全损伤状态——这是碎裂，不是偏转。该拓扑要求对靶材材料属性——孔隙率、强度与状态方程——进行先遣表征，因为最优standoff距离是这些参数的函数。没有先遣数据，standoff烧蚀是盲部署：太近则小行星碎裂；太远则冲量不足。
