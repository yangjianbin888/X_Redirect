**01. Impact Kinetic Energy to Vaporization: The Absolute Deflection Threshold**

**Part 1: Current State** (as of November 2026)

NASA's Double Asteroid Redirection Test (DART) demonstrated in 2022 that a kinetic impactor can measurably alter an asteroid's orbital period. The momentum enhancement factor β—the ratio of asteroid momentum change to impactor momentum—was measured for the Dimorphos target, with values in the range β ≈ 2.2–4.9 depending on ejecta modeling assumptions . ESA's Hera mission is en route to characterize the DART crater and refine β scaling laws. This document acknowledges that progress. The physical deadlock addressed here is not deflection feasibility at the Dimorphos scale. It is the energy-density threshold above which the momentum-transfer mechanism itself ceases to exist.

❌ **Core Physical Deadlock**: Kinetic deflection relies on momentum transfer through directed ejecta. The momentum enhancement factor β arises from material ejected at speeds exceeding the asteroid's escape velocity . When impact energy density at the coupling point exceeds the vaporization threshold of crustal silicates, the target material transitions from solid-fragment ejecta to vapor. Vapor expands isotropically; it does not carry directed momentum along the impact vector. The enhancement factor β collapses toward 1—the inelastic collision limit. Above this threshold, adding impactor mass or velocity does not scale deflection; it scales vaporization.

❌ **Engineering Misconception**: The assumption that β remains constant or increases with impact energy is physically invalid above the vaporization threshold. DART's β measurement (≈2.2–4.9 for Dimorphos) is a low-energy-density regime result . Laboratory and numerical studies confirm β depends on target porosity, strength, and boulder volume fraction, and declines when these parameters suppress crater growth . Extrapolating Dimorphos-scale β to higher impact energies violates the phase transition physics. A larger impactor against a larger asteroid does not preserve β; it crosses the vaporization threshold and destroys the momentum-transfer mechanism.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (Only Exit)**: The only physical exit from the vaporization deadlock is to abandon momentum-transfer deflection above the threshold and adopt energy-deposition topologies that operate below the fragmentation limit. Standoff nuclear ablation deposits energy into a surface layer thin enough to produce directed ejecta without bulk vaporization of the coupling volume . The blow-off momentum from X-ray ablation of SiO₂ surfaces has been modeled at energy densities ranging from 1.1 to 200 times the melt energy density, establishing lower bounds on deflection impulse as a function of deposition depth . This topology requires precursor characterization of the target's material properties—porosity, strength, and equation of state—because the optimal standoff distance is a function of these parameters. Without precursor data, standoff ablation is a blind deployment: too close and the asteroid fragments; too far and the impulse is insufficient.

**01. 撞击动能至汽化相变：绝对偏转阈值**

**第一部分：现状**（截止至2026年11月）

NASA的DART任务于2022年证明，动能撞击器可 measurably 改变小行星轨道周期。动量增强因子β——小行星动量变化与撞击器动量之比——在Dimorphos目标上被测量，取值区间约为 β ≈ 2.2–4.9，具体取决于抛射物建模假设 。ESA的Hera任务正在途中，将对DART撞击坑进行表征并修正β标度律。本文承认上述进展。此处指出的物理死锁不是Dimorphos尺度的偏转可行性问题。它是能量密度超过某一阈值后，动量传递机制本身不再存在的物理死锁。

❌ **核心物理死锁**：动能偏转依赖通过定向抛射物实现的动量传递。动量增强因子β来源于以超过小行星逃逸速度抛射的物质 。当耦合点的撞击能量密度超过地壳硅酸盐的汽化阈值时，靶材从固体碎片抛射转变为蒸汽。蒸汽各向同性膨胀；它不携带沿撞击矢量方向的定向动量。增强因子β坍缩至1——非弹性碰撞极限。在该阈值之上，增加撞击器质量或速度不缩放偏转；它缩放汽化。

❌ **工程认知误区**：假设β随撞击能量保持恒定或增加，在汽化阈值之上物理上无效。DART的β测量值（Dimorphos的β ≈ 2.2–4.9）是低能量密度区间的结果 。实验室与数值研究确认，β依赖于靶材孔隙率、强度与巨石体积分数，并在这些参数抑制撞击坑生长时下降 。将Dimorphos尺度的β外推至更高撞击能量违反相变物理。更大的撞击器撞击更大的小行星不保持β；它穿越汽化阈值并摧毁动量传递机制。

✅ **系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）**：汽化死锁的唯一物理生路，是放弃阈值之上的动量传递偏转，采用在碎裂极限以下运行的能量沉积拓扑。Standoff核烧蚀将能量沉积到足够薄的表层，以产生定向抛射物而不使耦合体积发生体汽化 。SiO₂表面的X射线烧蚀吹离动量已在1.1至200倍熔化能量密度的沉积能量范围内被建模，建立了偏转冲量随沉积深度变化的下界 。该拓扑要求对靶材材料属性——孔隙率、强度与状态方程——进行先遣表征，因为最优standoff距离是这些参数的函数。没有先遣数据，standoff烧蚀是盲部署：太近则小行星碎裂；太远则冲量不足。
