**❌ The Root Deadlock:**

A tokamak disruption is a physical cascade with a fixed sequence: thermal quench, current quench, runaway electron avalanche. The community treats prediction and avoidance as primary defense. This is physically incomplete as a sole strategy.

The seed is formed during the thermal quench, not after. Hot-tail electrons—those in the high-energy tail of the pre-disruption Maxwellian that escape collisional slowing-down as the plasma cools—provide the seed population that the avalanche subsequently amplifies . The thermal quench timescale in reactor-relevant plasmas is on the order of milliseconds. The seed formation occurs *within* that window, not before it.

Once the current quench begins, the avalanche mechanism takes over. The Rosenbluth-Putvinski growth rate establishes that the runaway population grows exponentially when the induced electric field exceeds the critical field \(E_c\) . The growth rate is proportional to \((E/E_c - 1)\). During current quench, the induced electric field is large—of order several \(E_c\)—and the avalanche exponent is correspondingly large.

The wall impact energy is not uniform. It is a localized deposition event. Damage threshold analysis for tungsten under 100 MeV electron impact gives a melting threshold of approximately **30 MJ/m²** for bare tungsten divertor structures . For 300 MeV electrons, the threshold drops by 13–47% . The question is not whether the wall can absorb the energy. It is whether the beam can be deconfined before it concentrates.

**❌ The Engineering Illusion:**

The reflex is shattered pellet injection (SPI). Fire a cryogenic pellet, shatter it, radiate the energy away. ITER’s baseline mitigation is SPI. The physics is valid in principle. The illusion is in the timing assumption.

SPI must assimilate material into the plasma *before* the thermal quench completes. The pellet travels at speeds exceeding 500 m/s, and the mitigation timescale is on the order of 1–2 ms from firing to plasma interaction . To achieve injection within 10 ms of a detected disruption, the injector must be positioned as close to the plasma as engineering constraints permit . This is a geometric constraint, not a control-loop constraint. Faster diagnostics do not move the injector closer.

The second illusion is assimilation depth. At full plasma current, the material that does not penetrate to the core does not radiate the core energy. It cools the edge, changes the current profile, and may *seed* additional runaways rather than suppress them. ITER modeling indicates that RE avoidance may require raising electron density by a factor of **20–40 or more** . The mass throughput required for this density rise at 15 MA is not obviously compatible with the port area available for pellet injection.

**✅ The Strategic Reconstruction (The Only Exit):**

The only physically coherent exit is to accept that some disruptions will occur and to engineer the *consequence*. Two topologies survive first-principles scrutiny for reactor-relevant devices:

**Topology A: Passive runaway electron mitigation coil (REMC).**

SPARC will deploy a passively driven REMC that seeds MHD instabilities during current quench, enhancing RE transport to the wall before the avalanche can concentrate the beam . The coil is driven by the plasma current decay itself—no external trigger, no actuator latency. SPARC simulations show that with Ne-only massive gas injection (MGI), a runaway plateau of approximately **5.5 MA** forms; with the REMC, complete suppression of the RE current is obtained . The physical constraint is the coil L/R time constant: it must be shorter than the runaway plateau formation time to act before the beam concentrates. For SPARC-class geometry, this requires low-inductance, high-conductivity coil structures.

**Topology B: Two-stage SPI with deuterium pre-injection followed by high-Z dopant.**

ITER modeling indicates that staggered injection—hydrogen SPI followed by neon or neon-mixed SPI—is a promising scheme for RE avoidance by slowing hot-tail seed electrons with moderate dilution cooling before the high-Z injection radiates the thermal energy . The physical constraint is the energy balance after thermal quench: maintaining \(E/E_c\) below the avalanche threshold with massive deuterium deposition requires that the post-injection electron temperature remain in the collisional regime. If the plasma cools too rapidly, the induced electric field rises faster than the avalanche threshold can be managed.

Neither topology eliminates the disruption. They shift the failure mode from vessel breach to controlled energy dispersal.

**❌ 核心物理死锁：**

托卡马克破裂是一个具有固定序列的物理级联：热淬灭、电流淬灭、逃逸电子雪崩。聚变界将预测与规避作为首要防线。作为唯一策略，这在物理上是不完整的。

种子形成于热淬灭期间，而非之后。热尾电子——破裂前麦克斯韦分布高能尾部中在等离子体冷却时逃脱碰撞减速的那部分电子——提供雪崩随后放大的种子布居 。反应堆相关等离子体中的热淬灭时间尺度为毫秒量级。种子形成发生在该窗口*之内*，而非之前。

一旦电流淬灭开始，雪崩机制接管。Rosenbluth-Putvinski 增长速率确立：当感应电场超过临界场 \(E_c\) 时，逃逸电子布居呈指数增长 。增长速率正比于 \((E/E_c - 1)\)。在电流淬灭期间，感应电场很大——为 \(E_c\) 的数倍量级——雪崩指数相应很大。

壁撞击能量不是均匀的。它是局部化沉积事件。100 MeV 电子撞击下钨的损伤阈值分析给出裸钨偏滤器结构的熔化阈值约为 **30 MJ/m²** 。对于 300 MeV 电子，阈值下降 13–47% 。问题不是壁能否吸收能量。是束流能否在集中之前被去约束。

**❌ 工程认知误区：**

条件反射是碎弹丸注入（SPI）。发射低温弹丸，击碎它，辐射掉能量。ITER 的基线缓解是 SPI。原理上物理有效。幻觉在于时序假设。

SPI 必须在热淬灭完成*之前*将材料同化入等离子体。弹丸以超过 500 m/s 的速度飞行，从点火到等离子体相互作用的缓解时间尺度为 1–2 ms 量级 。要在探测到破裂后 10 ms 内实现注入，注入器必须尽可能靠近等离子体布置，受工程约束限制 。这是几何约束，不是控制回路约束。更快的诊断不会让注入器更近。

第二个幻觉是同化深度。在全等离子体电流下，未穿透至核心的材料不辐射核心能量。它冷却边缘，改变电流剖面，并且可能*播种*额外逃逸电子而非抑制它们。ITER 建模表明，避免逃逸电子可能需要将电子密度提高 **20–40 倍或更多** 。在 15 MA 下实现该密度升高所需的质量通量，与弹丸注入可用端口面积之间的兼容性并不显然。

**✅ 系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）：**

唯一物理自洽的出口是接受某些破裂将会发生，并工程化*后果*。两个拓扑通过反应堆相关装置的第一性原理审查：

**拓扑 A：被动逃逸电子缓解线圈（REMC）。**

SPARC 将部署一个被动驱动的 REMC，在电流淬灭期间播种 MHD 不稳定性，在雪崩集中束流之前增强逃逸电子向壁的输运 。线圈由等离子体电流衰减本身驱动——无外部触发，无执行器延迟。SPARC 模拟显示，仅用氖大规模气体注入（MGI）时形成约 **5.5 MA** 的逃逸平台；使用 REMC 时获得逃逸电子电流的完全抑制 。物理约束是线圈 L/R 时间常数：它必须短于逃逸平台形成时间，才能在束流集中之前动作。对 SPARC 级几何，这需要低电感、高电导率线圈结构。

**拓扑 B：氘预注入后接高 Z 掺杂的两级 SPI。**

ITER 建模表明，交错注入——氢 SPI 后接氖或氖混合 SPI——是一种有前景的逃逸电子避免方案：先以适度稀释冷却减缓热尾种子电子，再由高 Z 注入辐射热能量 。物理约束是热淬灭后的能量平衡：在大规模氘沉积下维持 \(E/E_c\) 低于雪崩阈值，要求注入后电子温度保持在碰撞主导区间。如果等离子体冷却过快，感应电场上升速度超过雪崩阈值可被管理的速度。

两个拓扑都没有消除破裂。它们将失效模式从真空室穿透转向受控能量耗散。
