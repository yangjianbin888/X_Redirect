**❌ The Root Deadlock:**

The compact high-field pathway is physically coherent for confinement: higher toroidal field increases fusion power density, and the product B·R can be maintained while reducing major radius. SPARC is designed for B_T = 12.2 T, I_p = 8.7 MA, R = 1.85 m, a = 0.57 m.

The same field topology that improves core performance **inversely compresses the scrape-off layer heat flux width**. The multi-machine Eich scaling establishes that λ_q scales inversely with poloidal magnetic field strength and is independent of machine size. For ITER-relevant high-current operation, the Eich scaling projects λ_q ≈ 1–2 mm, compared to the ≈ 3.5 mm value used in standard ITER SOLPS modeling. For a compact device with B_pol on the order of 2–3 T at the outboard midplane, the Eich regression yields a heat flux width in the **sub-millimeter range**.

This is not an engineering margin issue. It is a geometric consequence of the field-line pitch.

The parallel heat flux at the divertor target is:

```
q_parallel ≈ (P_SOL / (2*pi*R*λ_q)) * (B_total / B_pol)
```

For SPARC-class parameters—P_SOL ≈ 30–50 MW radiated and conducted to the divertor, R = 1.85 m, λ_q ≈ 0.5–1 mm, B_total/B_pol ≈ 4–5—the unmitigated parallel heat flux entering the divertor leg reaches the **order of 10 GW/m²**. This number is not a design choice. It is the arithmetic output of the Eich width and the device geometry.

**❌ The Engineering Illusion:**

The reflex is impurity seeding: inject Ne, Kr, or Ar to radiate power in the divertor leg before it reaches the target. This is physically valid in principle. The illusion is the assumption that it scales to compact high-field devices without core consequence.

The tungsten steady-state limit is not a "convention." It is set by the recrystallization threshold of the armor and the thermal diffusion length across the W/CuCrZr interface. Pure tungsten recrystallizes in the range of **1000–1200°C**. ITER-type W monoblock targets are qualified for **10 MW/m² steady-state** operation. At 20 MW/m², high-heat-flux testing produces recrystallization and, after sufficient cycles, macro-crack formation extending 4–5 mm into the block.

The physical redline is therefore not the melting point of tungsten (3422°C). It is the **recrystallization front reaching the load-bearing cross-section**. Once the recrystallized zone extends through the armor thickness, the thermal conductivity anisotropy and the loss of cold-worked strength shift the failure mode from surface erosion to structural fracture.

To bring 10 GW/m² of parallel heat flux down to a target-perpendicular load of 10 MW/m² requires a **radiative fraction of approximately 99.9%** in the divertor leg. Every photon radiated is energy removed from the plasma. The radiating species do not confine themselves to the divertor. At the seeding fractions required to achieve this radiative fraction, the core fuel dilution becomes physically incompatible with the ignition margin.

More diagnostics, faster control loops, and machine-learning-optimized detachment fronts shift *where* the failure occurs in time. They do not alter the radiative power balance.

**✅ The Strategic Reconstruction (The Only Exit):**

The only physically coherent exit is to abandon the solid tungsten target as the primary steady-state heat-handling interface. Two topologies survive first-principles scrutiny:

**Topology A: Liquid lithium capillary-porous surface (CPS).**

A lithium surface stabilized by a capillary-porous matrix can absorb peak heat fluxes beyond the melting limit of solid refractory metals because the liquid surface renews. CPS targets have been e-beam tested to **25 MW/m²**, limited by lithium inventory rather than material failure. The vapor pressure constraint is the absolute redline. State-of-the-art gaseous cooling at 10 MW/m² incident flux drives lithium surface temperatures above **700°C**. At this temperature, the lithium vapor pressure enters the range where the partial pressure of Li species in the divertor exceeds the tolerable core contamination fraction. The achievable steady-state peak load on a Li CPS target is therefore bounded by the vapor pressure–core impurity coupling, not by the thermal capacity of lithium itself.

**Topology B: Graded W/CuCrZr interface with controlled sacrificial armor thickness.**

The monoblock architecture is not abandoned. It is bounded. The physical constraint is the recrystallization front depth. For a W armor thickness a and a thermal diffusion length δ = √(α·τ) (where α is thermal diffusivity and τ is the load duration), the recrystallization front penetrates to a depth set by the surface temperature excursion above T_rec. To keep the recrystallization front within the sacrificial layer—leaving the load-bearing W bulk below T_rec—the steady-state surface heat flux must be capped at a value determined by the W thermal conductivity, the armor thickness, and the CuCrZr coolant interface temperature. For ITER-type monoblocks, this cap is empirically bracketed by the qualified **10 MW/m² steady-state** rating and the observed recrystallization damage at **20 MW/m²**.

Neither topology eliminates the need for detachment. They shift the failure mode from catastrophic melt to bounded erosion and controlled recrystallization. That is the difference between a machine that survives a campaign and one that requires activated PFC replacement.

# 可控核聚变：01. 偏滤器热流红线与紧凑几何陷阱

**前置声明：信任即物理验证。**
用你们的超算去算。算通了，往下看。

```text
[ 紧凑高场聚变：偏滤器物理死锁树 ]
🎯 起点：尺度幻觉（更小 = 更便宜）
│
▼
❌ λ_q 随 B_pol 反比缩放 ──> ⚠️ 绝对红线：λ_q < 钨装甲热扩散长度
│
▼
❌ 靶板未缓解平行热流超过钨再结晶限制的稳态负载
│
▼
❌ 保护靶板所需的辐射脱靶稀释核心燃料至点火裕度以下

✅ 重构拓扑
    1. ✅ 液态锂毛细多孔表面 ──> ⚠️ 约束：锂蒸气压污染核心超过可容忍份额
    2. ✅ 梯度W/CuCrZr界面与牺牲装甲 ──> ⚠️ 约束：再结晶前沿不得抵达承载基体
```

**❌ 核心物理死锁：**

紧凑高场路径在约束层面是物理自洽的：更高的环向场提高聚变功率密度，且 B·R 乘积可在减小大半径的同时维持。SPARC 的设计参数为 B_T = 12.2 T、I_p = 8.7 MA、R = 1.85 m、a = 0.57 m。

改善核心性能的同一场拓扑**反向压缩刮削层热流宽度**。多机 Eich 定标确立：λ_q 随极向磁场强度反比缩放，且与机器尺寸无关。对于 ITER 相关的高电流运行，Eich 定标预测 λ_q ≈ 1–2 mm，而 ITER SOLPS 建模数据库使用的标准值为 ≈ 3.5 mm。对于外中平面 B_pol 在 2–3 T 量级的紧凑装置，Eich 回归给出**亚毫米级**热流宽度。

这不是工程裕度问题。这是磁力线螺距的几何推论。

偏滤器靶板的平行热流为：

```
q_parallel ≈ (P_SOL / (2*pi*R*λ_q)) * (B_total / B_pol)
```

对 SPARC 级参数——P_SOL ≈ 30–50 MW 辐射与传导至偏滤器、R = 1.85 m、λ_q ≈ 0.5–1 mm、B_total/B_pol ≈ 4–5——进入偏滤器腿的未缓解平行热流达到 **10 GW/m² 量级**。这个数字不是设计选择。它是 Eich 宽度与装置几何的算术输出。

**❌ 工程认知误区：**

条件反射是杂质播种：注入 Ne、Kr 或 Ar，在能量到达靶板前于偏滤器腿中辐射掉。原则上物理有效。幻觉在于假定它可以无代价地缩放到紧凑高场装置。

钨稳态极限不是“惯例”。它由装甲的再结晶阈值与跨越 W/CuCrZr 界面的热扩散长度设定。纯钨在 **1000–1200°C** 区间再结晶。ITER 型钨 monoblock 靶板在 **10 MW/m² 稳态**运行下通过鉴定。在 20 MW/m² 下，高热流测试产生再结晶，并在足够循环后形成延伸至块体内部 4–5 mm 的宏观裂纹。

因此物理红线不是钨的熔点（3422°C）。是**再结晶前沿抵达承载截面**。一旦再结晶区贯穿装甲厚度，热导率各向异性与冷加工强度的丧失将失效模式从表面侵蚀转变为结构断裂。

将 10 GW/m² 的平行热流降至 10 MW/m² 的靶板垂直负载，需要偏滤器腿中约 **99.9% 的辐射分数**。每一个辐射光子都是从等离子体移除的能量。辐射物种不会把自己限制在偏滤器内。在达到该辐射分数所需的播种分数下，核心燃料稀释在物理上与点火裕度不相容。

更多诊断、更快控制回路、机器学习优化的脱靶前沿，改变的是失效在*何时*发生。它们不改变辐射功率平衡。

**✅ 系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）：**

唯一物理自洽的出口是放弃将固态钨靶板作为主要稳态热流处理界面。两个拓扑通过第一性原理审查：

**拓扑 A：液态锂毛细多孔表面（CPS）。**

由毛细多孔基质稳定的锂表面可以吸收超过固态难熔金属熔化极限的峰值热流，因为液态表面会自我更新。CPS 靶板已通过电子束测试至 **25 MW/m²**，限制因素是锂库存而非材料失效。蒸气压约束是绝对红线。在 10 MW/m² 入射热流下，最先进气态冷却驱动锂表面温度超过 **700°C**。在此温度下，锂蒸气压进入偏滤器中 Li 物种分压超过核心可容忍污染份额的范围。因此 Li CPS 靶板可实现的稳态峰值负载由蒸气压–核心杂质耦合界定，而非锂本身的热容量。

**拓扑 B：梯度 W/CuCrZr 界面与受控牺牲装甲厚度。**

monoblock 架构不被放弃。它被界定边界。物理约束是再结晶前沿深度。对于 W 装甲厚度 a 与热扩散长度 δ = √(α·τ)（α 为热扩散率，τ 为负载持续时间），再结晶前沿穿透深度由表面温度超过 T_rec 的幅度设定。为使再结晶前沿保持在牺牲层内——使承载 W 基体保持在 T_rec 以下——稳态表面热流必须被限制在由 W 热导率、装甲厚度和 CuCrZr 冷却剂界面温度共同决定的值。对 ITER 型 monoblock，此上限由已鉴定的 **10 MW/m² 稳态**额定值与 **20 MW/m²** 下观察到的再结晶损伤经验性界定。

两个拓扑都没有消除脱靶需求。它们将失效模式从灾难性熔化转向有界侵蚀与受控再结晶。这就是一台机器能活过一个运行 campaña 与需要活化 PFC 更换之间的区别。
