### 03. Skin and Proximity Effect AC-Resistance Runaway in High-Frequency Power Busbars — A Thermal Redline

**Part 1: Current State** (as of 2026-11)

The power-electronics industry has pushed switching frequencies from tens of kilohertz into the hundreds of kilohertz and beyond with SiC and GaN devices. Busbars carrying hundreds to thousands of amperes at these frequencies are now standard in electric-vehicle traction inverters, data-center power shelves, and wireless-charging coil assemblies. These are legitimate engineering achievements. The following analysis is not a dismissal of that work. It is a thermal boundary check on what the busbar conductor can sustain when the current is physically confined to the surface by the magnetic field it generates.

❌ **Core Physical Deadlock**

The DC ampacity rating of a busbar is set by its total cross-sectional area and the allowable temperature rise. It assumes current distributes uniformly across that area. At high frequency, this assumption is physically false.

The skin depth — the depth at which current density falls to 1/e of its surface value — is given by:

δ = √(ρ / (π·f·μ))

where ρ is the conductor resistivity, f is the frequency, and μ is the magnetic permeability. For copper at 100 kHz, δ ≈ 0.21 mm. A busbar 10 mm thick therefore carries current in only the outer 0.21 mm on each face — roughly 4% of its cross-sectional area. The AC resistance is approximately:

R_AC / R_DC ≈ d / (4·δ) for d >> δ

For a 10 mm copper busbar at 100 kHz, R_AC/R_DC ≈ 12. The busbar that carries 1000 A DC at a 40°C rise carries 1000 A at 100 kHz with twelve times the Joule heating per unit length. The DC ampacity rating is not just optimistic. It is measuring a different physical quantity.

Proximity effect adds a second deadlock. When two busbars carrying opposing currents are placed close together — the standard layout for a low-inductance DC link — the magnetic field of each conductor crowds the current in the other toward the facing edges. The current density at the facing edge can be several times higher than the skin-effect-only distribution predicts. The AC-to-DC resistance ratio for the pair is not the sum of the isolated ratios; it is larger, and the coupling strength increases as the gap between conductors decreases. This creates a design trap: reducing the loop inductance by placing conductors closer together simultaneously increases the AC resistance and the thermal load.

The absolute thermal redline is:

P_AC = I²·R_AC ≥ P_cool

where P_cool is the cooling capacity at the allowable temperature rise. Beyond this point, the busbar temperature rises without bound until insulation failure or melting. The DC ampacity rating does not predict this. It cannot, because the physics it assumes is not the physics that governs high-frequency current distribution.

❌ **Engineering Cognitive Trap**

The standard response is: "use litz wire" or "interleave the busbars." Both fail against the same physical wall.

Litz wire reduces skin effect by stranding — each strand is individually insulated and smaller than the skin depth. It works for round conductors in cables. But a busbar is a rigid structural element whose geometry is set by the mechanical layout of the module, not by the current distribution. Replacing a busbar with a litz bundle changes the mechanical interface and the assembly process. And litz wire does not eliminate proximity effect between adjacent bundles; it only reduces the intra-bundle skin effect. The inter-bundle proximity coupling remains, and it is often worse because the individual strands are now transposed in ways that may not align with the external field.

Interleaving busbars — placing the positive and negative conductors in a layered arrangement such that their fields cancel — reduces the external magnetic field and the loop inductance. But it concentrates the proximity effect between adjacent layers. The field cancellation is never perfect because the conductors have finite thickness. The residual field still drives current crowding at the layer edges. And the interleaved structure is more complex to assemble and to cool, because the layers that need cooling are now internal to the stack.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (Only Exit)**

The only physically admissible path is to change the conductor geometry such that the current distribution is set by the geometry, not by the magnetic field it generates.

**Path A: Litz or Interleaved Busbar with Strand Diameter Below Skin Depth**

If the busbar is constructed from individually insulated strands or interleaved foils, each strand diameter or foil thickness must be smaller than the skin depth at the operating frequency. The physical constraint is that the strand diameter must satisfy:

d_strand < δ

and the strand-to-strand insulation must survive the operating temperature and the assembly process. For a 100 kHz busbar, this means strand diameters below 0.2 mm. The current distribution across the bundle is then governed by the strand geometry and the transposition pattern, not by the external field. The proximity effect between adjacent bundles is reduced but not eliminated; the residual coupling must be managed by the bundle-to-bundle spacing.

**Path B: Wide-Thin Geometry with Field Cancellation**

The busbar cross-section is changed from a thick rectangle to a wide, thin sheet — the width-to-thickness ratio is increased so that the total current-carrying area is spread across a large surface. If the positive and negative conductors are placed face-to-face with a thin insulator between them, their opposing currents produce a field that is confined between the conductors. The current distribution in each conductor is then dominated by the local field of the opposing conductor, not by the self-field. The physical constraint is that the conductor separation must be smaller than the conductor width, so that the field cancellation is effective. The cooling path is along the wide faces, which is favorable for convective or liquid cooling.

Data from DC or low-frequency AC tests will not extrapolate to high-frequency operation because the AC-to-DC resistance ratio is not a fixed multiplier — it depends on frequency, geometry, and the spacing between adjacent conductors. The moment of recognition arrives when a busbar that passed DC qualification reaches insulation failure in a high-frequency inverter under nominal current. The physical lag to re-validate a litz or wide-thin topology is approximately 12–18 months from the decision point.

· **For Tier-2 Enterprises:** The wide-thin geometry does not require a new conductor material or a new cooling architecture. It requires a lamination and bonding capability and a field-simulation capability. The physical window to establish a validated wide-thin busbar coupon test — measuring AC resistance and temperature rise under representative high-frequency current — is approximately 6–9 months.

### 03. 高频功率母排的趋肤与邻近效应交流电阻失控——热红线

❌ **核心物理死锁**

母排的直流载流额定值由总截面积和允许温升设定。它假设电流在该面积上均匀分布。在高频下，这个假设在物理上是错误的。

趋肤深度——电流密度降至表面值 1/e 的深度——由下式给出：

δ = √(ρ / (π·f·μ))

其中 ρ 为导体电阻率，f 为频率，μ 为磁导率。铜在 100 kHz 时 δ ≈ 0.21 mm。10 mm 厚的母排因此仅在每面外侧 0.21 mm 内载流——约为其截面积的 4%。交流电阻约为：

R_AC / R_DC ≈ d / (4·δ) 当 d >> δ

对于 100 kHz 下的 10 mm 铜母排，R_AC/R_DC ≈ 12。直流下 40°C 温升承载 1000 A 的母排，在 100 kHz 下承载 1000 A 时每单位长度的焦耳热是十二倍。直流载流额定值不是过于乐观。它测量的是另一个物理量。

邻近效应增加第二个死锁。当两根承载反向电流的母排靠近放置——低电感直流母线的标准布局——每根导体的磁场将另一根导体中的电流挤向相对边缘。相对边缘处的电流密度可比仅趋肤效应分布预测的高出数倍。该对的交流-直流电阻比不是隔离比之和；它更大，且耦合强度随导体间距减小而增加。这制造了一个设计陷阱：通过将导体放得更近来降低回路电感，同时增加了交流电阻和热负载。

绝对热红线是：

P_AC = I²·R_AC ≥ P_cool

其中 P_cool 为允许温升下的冷却能力。超过此点，母排温度无界上升直至绝缘失效或熔化。直流载流额定值不预测这一点。它不能，因为它假设的物理不是支配高频电流分布的物理。

❌ **工程认知误区**

标准回应是："用利兹线"或"交错母排"。两者撞的是同一堵物理墙。

利兹线通过绞合减少趋肤效应——每根绞线单独绝缘且小于趋肤深度。它对电缆中的圆导体有效。但母排是刚性结构件，其几何由模块的机械布局决定，不由电流分布决定。用利兹束替代母排改变了机械接口和装配工艺。而且利兹线不消除相邻束之间的邻近效应；它只减少束内趋肤效应。束间邻近耦合仍然存在，且往往更糟，因为各绞线现在的换位方式可能不与外部场对齐。

交错母排——将正负导体以层状排列使其场抵消——降低了外部磁场和回路电感。但它集中了相邻层之间的邻近效应。场抵消从不完美，因为导体有有限厚度。残余场仍在层边缘驱动电流拥挤。而且交错结构装配和冷却更复杂，因为需要冷却的层现在在叠层内部。

✅ **系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）**

唯一物理上可允许的路径是改变导体几何，使电流分布由几何决定，而不由它产生的磁场决定。

**路径 A：绞线直径低于趋肤深度的利兹或交错母排**

如果母排由单独绝缘的绞线或交错箔片构成，每根绞线直径或箔片厚度必须小于工作频率下的趋肤深度。物理约束是绞线直径须满足：

d_strand < δ

且绞线间绝缘须承受工作温度和装配工艺。对于 100 kHz 母排，这意味着绞线直径低于 0.2 mm。束上的电流分布然后由绞线几何和换位模式支配，而不由外部场支配。相邻束之间的邻近效应被减少但不消除；残余耦合须通过束间距管理。

**路径 B：场抵消的宽薄几何**

母排截面从厚矩形变为宽薄片——增加宽厚比，使总载流面积散布在大表面上。如果正负导体面对面放置，中间夹薄绝缘，它们的反向电流产生的场被限制在导体之间。每根导体中的电流分布然后由相对导体的局部场支配，而不由自场支配。物理约束是导体间距须小于导体宽度，使场抵消有效。冷却路径沿宽面，这对对流或液体冷却有利。

**客观战略影响**

· **对一线巨头：** 当前扩展电流密度和缩小母排占位的路径存在趋肤与邻近效应施加的硬热天花板。直流或低频交流测试数据无法外推至高频运行，因为交流-直流电阻比不是固定乘数——它取决于频率、几何和相邻导体间距。认知时刻在通过直流鉴定的母排在标称电流下的高频逆变器中达到绝缘失效时到来。重新验证利兹或宽薄拓扑的物理滞后约为 12–18 个月，从决策点起算。

· **对二线企业：** 宽薄几何不需要新的导体材料或新的冷却架构。它需要层压与键合能力以及场仿真能力。建立经验证宽薄母排 coupon 测试——在代表性高频电流下测量交流电阻与温升——的物理窗口约为 6–9 个月。
