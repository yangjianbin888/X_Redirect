❌ **Core Physical Deadlock:**

Fixed-frequency transmon architectures operate within a finite spectral window defined by the qubit anharmonicity. For typical transmons, the |1⟩ → |2⟩ transition is approximately 200–300 MHz below the |0⟩ → |1⟩ transition. To avoid leakage into the |2⟩ state during microwave driving, qubit frequencies must be spaced within this anharmonicity window. The usable band is therefore bounded.

Fabrication uncertainty imposes a second constraint. Josephson junction critical current reproducibility is limited by tunnel barrier oxidation control and junction area definition. State-of-the-art fabrication achieves approximately ±1–2% frequency reproducibility at 5 GHz, corresponding to ±50–100 MHz spread . When N qubits are fabricated, the actual frequencies deviate from the designed values. As N grows, the spectral density within the anharmonicity window increases, and the probability that two qubits (or a qubit and a collision condition involving higher levels or couplers) land within the collision threshold rises.

The collision itself is not a calibration error. It is a resonance condition: a microwave drive centered at qubit i‘s frequency also has non-zero spectral weight at qubit j’s frequency if the detuning is small compared to the Rabi frequency. The drive excites Rabi oscillations in qubit j. The phase of those oscillations depends on the drive phase and the detuning. When multiple qubits are driven simultaneously, the Rabi oscillations interfere . The resulting error is coherent and correlated across the array.

This is the architectural inverse of Deadlock 01. In trapped ions, the coupling to motional modes is unavoidable and scales against you. In fixed-frequency superconducting qubits, the coupling that enables two-qubit gates (capacitive or coupler-mediated) also mediates microwave crosstalk. You cannot strengthen the former without strengthening the latter. The always-on coupling is the physical resource and the physical liability simultaneously.

❌ **Engineering Misconception:**

“Better calibration” or “crosstalk-aware compilation” operates at the software and pulse-sequence layer. These methods mitigate the observable error by scheduling non-interfering gates sequentially or by pre-distorting the control pulses. They do not change the physical collision probability. When the number of qubits grows to the point where every qubit has a non-negligible spectral overlap with at least one other qubit, serialization becomes the only error-free schedule. Serialization inflates circuit depth. Inflated circuit depth increases exposure to decoherence (Deadlock 01) and thermal load (Deadlock 02).

The fault-tolerance threshold condition requires crosstalk error probability to satisfy P_crosstalk < ε_f², where ε_f is the independent physical gate error rate . For ε_f ~ 10⁻³, this demands P_crosstalk < 10⁻⁶. At the spectral densities achievable with current fabrication reproducibility, maintaining this bound across a large array requires frequency spacing that exceeds the anharmonicity window. The architecture is caught between two physical walls: spacing too close causes crosstalk, spacing too wide causes leakage.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (The Only Physical Path):**

Three levers exist, each with a physical tax.

**Lever 1: Model-based frequency optimization.** Assign qubit frequencies to minimize the maximum crosstalk error across all pairs. This has been demonstrated on 49-qubit arrays with mean simultaneous single-qubit gate fidelity of 99.96% . The tax: the optimization problem scales combinatorially with N, and the required fabrication precision increases because the margin for assignment error shrinks. At N = 1000, the frequency assignment must be accurate to within a fraction of the collision threshold, which is already below the fabrication reproducibility limit.

**Lever 2: Crosstalk transition suppression (CTS) pulse shaping.** Shape the microwave envelope to minimize spectral energy at collision transition frequencies . The tax: pulse lengthening. A shaped pulse that suppresses spectral leakage by an order of magnitude typically requires 2–5× longer duration. Longer single-qubit gates increase exposure to T2 decay and to the always-on ZZ coupling. The net fidelity gain narrows as T2 shrinks (Deadlock 01 coupling).

**Lever 3: Tunable couplers or tunable qubits.** Break the fixed-frequency constraint. The tax: reintroduces flux control lines, flux noise, and the control wiring density problem. Every tunable element is another source of 1/f noise and another line that must be routed through the mK stage (Deadlock 02). The architectural fix for Deadlock 03 is a direct injection of Deadlock 01 and Deadlock 02.

**Conclusion: The fixed-frequency transmon architecture faces a spectral crowding tax that scales with N. The three compensation levers each trade one physical liability for another. No lever eliminates the collision probability without either increasing circuit depth (exposing Deadlock 01) or reintroducing control complexity (exposing Deadlock 02). The fault-tolerance threshold condition P_crosstalk < ε_f² becomes increasingly difficult to satisfy as N grows, not because of engineering shortfall, but because the spectral resource is finite and the fabrication uncertainty is physical.**

❌ **核心物理死锁：**

固定频率 transmon 架构在由比特非谐性定义的有限频谱窗口内运行。对于典型 transmon，|1⟩ → |2⟩ 跃迁比 |0⟩ → |1⟩ 跃迁低约 200–300 MHz。为避免微波驱动期间泄漏至 |2⟩ 态，比特频率必须在此非谐性窗口内间隔分布。可用频带因此有界。

制造不确定性施加第二约束。约瑟夫森结临界电流复现性受隧道势垒氧化控制和结面积定义限制。当前最先进制造在 5 GHz 下实现约 ±1–2% 频率复现性，对应 ±50–100 MHz 展宽 。当 N 个比特被制造时，实际频率偏离设计值。随 N 增长，非谐性窗口内的频谱密度增加，两个比特（或比特与涉及更高能级或耦合器的碰撞条件）落入碰撞阈值内的概率上升。

碰撞本身不是校准误差。它是共振条件：以比特 i 频率为中心的微波驱动，在比特 j 频率处也有非零频谱权重——若失谐小于拉比频率。驱动在比特 j 中激发拉比振荡。这些振荡的相位取决于驱动相位和失谐。当多个比特同时被驱动时，拉比振荡相互干涉 。产生的错误是相干的且在阵列中关联。

这是死锁 01 的架构镜像。在离子阱中，与运动模的耦合不可避免且反向标度。在固定频率超导比特中，使能双比特门的耦合（电容或耦合器中介）也中介微波串扰。你不能在加强前者的同时不加强后者。常开耦合同时是物理资源和物理负债。

❌ **工程认知误区：**

“更好的校准”或“串扰感知编译”作用于软件和脉冲序列层。这些方法通过串行调度非干涉门或预失真控制脉冲来缓解可观测错误。它们不改变物理碰撞概率。当比特数增长到每个比特都与至少一个其他比特有不可忽略的频谱重叠时，串行化成为唯一无错误调度。串行化膨胀电路深度。膨胀的电路深度增加对退相干（死锁 01）和热负载（死锁 02）的暴露。

容错阈值条件要求串扰错误概率满足 P_crosstalk < ε_f²，其中 ε_f 是独立物理门错误率 。对于 ε_f ~ 10⁻³，这要求 P_crosstalk < 10⁻⁶。在当前制造复现性可达到的频谱密度下，在大阵列中维持此界限所需的频率间隔超过非谐性窗口。架构被夹在两堵物理墙之间：间隔太近导致串扰，间隔太宽导致泄漏。

✅ **系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）：**

三个杠杆存在，每个都有物理税。

**杠杆 1：模型化频率优化。** 分配比特频率以最小化所有比特对的最大串扰错误。已在 49 比特阵列上演示，并行单比特门平均保真度 99.96% 。税：优化问题随 N 组合爆炸，且所需制造精度上升，因为分配误差的裕度收窄。在 N = 1000 时，频率分配须精确到碰撞阈值的一小部分，而该阈值已低于制造复现性极限。

**杠杆 2：串扰跃迁抑制（CTS）脉冲整形。** 整形微波包络以最小化碰撞跃迁频率处的频谱能量 。税：脉冲展宽。将频谱泄漏抑制一个数量级的整形脉冲通常需要 2–5 倍时长。更长的单比特门增加对 T2 衰减和常开 ZZ 耦合的暴露。净保真度增益随 T2 缩短（死锁 01 耦合）而收窄。

**杠杆 3：可调耦合器或可调比特。** 打破固定频率约束。税：重新引入磁通控制线、磁通噪声和控制布线密度问题。每个可调元件都是另一个 1/f 噪声源，另一条必须穿过 mK 级的线（死锁 02）。死锁 03 的架构修复是死锁 01 和死锁 02 的直接注入。

**结论：固定频率 transmon 架构面临随 N 标度的频谱拥挤税。三个代偿杠杆各自用一种物理负债交换另一种。没有杠杆能在不增加电路深度（暴露死锁 01）或不重新引入控制复杂度（暴露死锁 02）的情况下消除碰撞概率。容错阈值条件 P_crosstalk < ε_f² 随 N 增长而日益难以满足，不是因为工程短板，而是因为频谱资源有限且制造不确定性是物理的。**

死锁 03 是死锁 01 的频谱终点和死锁 02 的控制层放大器。碰撞概率强制串行化。串行化膨胀电路深度。膨胀的电路深度增加每个逻辑周期的门操作数，每一个都暴露于死锁 01 的 T2 极限和死锁 02 的热负载。同时，代偿杠杆——可调耦合器、CTS 脉冲整形、频率优化——各自要求额外控制线或更长脉冲。额外控制线增加 mK 级布线密度，注入死锁 02 无法提取的热负载。更长脉冲增加对常开 ZZ 耦合的暴露，而 ZZ 耦合本身就是一个串扰通道。

**死锁 03 强制串行化。串行化放大死锁 01 和 02。三角闭合。**
