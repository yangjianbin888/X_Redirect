❌ **Core Physical Deadlock:**

Two-level systems (TLS) are atomic-scale structural defects at amorphous interfaces—the Josephson tunnel barrier (AlOx), the substrate-metal interface, and the metal-air surface—that possess double-well potential energy landscapes. At millikelvin temperatures, only the lowest two levels of each double well are accessible, producing a quantum two-level system that couples to electric fields .

The standard tunneling model describes the TLS density of states as approximately constant in energy: the number of TLS per unit energy per unit volume is roughly independent of transition frequency. This means the TLS spectral density spans the entire microwave band. The qubit operating frequency will always overlap with a population of TLS whose transition energy matches the qubit energy.

When a TLS is resonantly coupled to the qubit, the qubit can transfer its energy to the TLS. The TLS then relaxes through phonon emission or other dissipation channels. The qubit state is lost. This is an energy relaxation channel: it suppresses T1. The relaxation rate scales as:

Γ_TLS ∝ n_TLS · |p|²

where n_TLS is the density of TLS at the qubit frequency and |p|² is the squared electric dipole matrix element of the TLS-qubit coupling, weighted by the TLS participation ratio in the qubit’s electric field volume.

The participation ratio is the critical metric: it quantifies how much of the qubit‘s electric field energy resides in amorphous materials . A qubit design that concentrates its field in the substrate bulk (low-loss crystalline Si) and minimizes field overlap with the AlOx junction barrier and metal-oxide interfaces will have a lower participation ratio and therefore a lower TLS-induced relaxation rate. This is precisely what the tantalum-on-silicon platform achieves: the Ta oxide has lower microwave loss than Nb or Al oxides, and high-resistivity Si has lower bulk loss than sapphire .

But the participation ratio cannot be driven to zero. The Josephson junction requires a tunnel barrier. The barrier is amorphous. The qubit’s electric field must overlap with the junction to achieve the Josephson nonlinearity. The minimum participation ratio is bounded by the junction geometry and the field distribution—both of which are constrained by the qubit’s required EJ/EC ratio and the fabrication process.

The tantalum-on-silicon platform is the current materials ceiling. It is not a solution to the TLS problem. It is an optimization within the TLS problem’s physical bounds.

❌ **Engineering Misconception:**

“Better fabrication” or “cleaner interfaces” operates on the TLS density prefactor, not on the fundamental structure of the amorphous barrier. Ultrahigh-vacuum deposition reduces hydrocarbon contamination and improves T2E . But the AlOx barrier remains amorphous. The two-level systems remain. The spectral density remains approximately constant in energy.

The fault-tolerant threshold condition requires the qubit relaxation rate to satisfy Γ_relax < ε_f / T_gate, where ε_f is the target gate error and T_gate is the gate duration. For ε_f ~ 10⁻⁴ and T_gate ~ 40 ns (the Princeton platform’s optimal gate time) , the required Γ_relax < 2.5 × 10³ s⁻¹, corresponding to T1 > 400 μs. The Princeton mean T1 of 0.45 ms satisfies this condition marginally. The maximum T1 of 1.68 ms satisfies it with margin.

But the mean T1 across 45 qubits is 0.45 ms . The distribution has a tail toward shorter lifetimes. As the qubit count scales to 10³ and beyond, the probability that at least one qubit in the array falls in the low-T1 tail approaches unity. The array’s error-correction performance is bounded by the worst qubit, not the best.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (The Only Physical Path):**

Three levers exist, each with a physical tax.

**Lever 1: Reduce TLS participation ratio through interface engineering.** Minimize the electric field overlap with amorphous regions. Use Ta instead of Nb for capacitor pads (Ta oxide has lower loss) . Use high-resistivity Si instead of sapphire (lower bulk loss) . Use UHV deposition to reduce hydrocarbon contamination at the junction interface . The tax: each material substitution has its own fabrication constraints and reproducibility challenges. The Ta-on-Si platform requires α-phase Ta growth on Si, which imposes specific deposition temperature and lattice matching conditions.

**Lever 2: Crystalline interface engineering.** Replace the amorphous AlOx barrier with an epitaxial crystalline barrier, or use van der Waals junctions with atomically clean interfaces . The tax: epitaxial growth requires lattice-matched substrates and high thermal budgets. The junction uniformity and reproducibility at scale are unproven. The crystalline barrier’s superconducting proximity effect and Josephson physics differ from AlOx and require re-validation of the entire qubit design.

**Lever 3: Spectral detuning.** Tune the qubit operating frequency away from spectral regions with high TLS density. TLS spectroscopy can map the spectral density, and the qubit can be biased to a “clean” frequency . The tax: frequency tunability requires flux lines or gate electrodes, which reintroduce Deadlock 03 (frequency collision, crosstalk, control wiring density). Fixed-frequency qubits cannot access this lever at all.

**Conclusion: The TLS defect absorption problem is a materials physics constraint, not an engineering shortfall. The tantalum-on-silicon platform demonstrates what is achievable within the current materials paradigm: mean T1 ~ 0.45 ms, maximum T1 ~ 1.68 ms. Extending this to arrays of 10³+ qubits with yield sufficient for fault tolerance requires either a materials breakthrough (crystalline barriers) that is not yet demonstrated at scale, or a system architecture that tolerates the low-T1 tail (active re-calibration, dynamic frequency allocation). Neither is available as an off-the-shelf solution.**

❌ **核心物理死锁：**

二能级系统（TLS）是非晶界面——约瑟夫森隧道势垒（AlOx）、衬底-金属界面、金属-空气表面——处的原子尺度结构缺陷，具有双势阱势能景观。在毫开尔文温度下，每个双势阱仅最低两个能级可及，产生耦合至电场的量子二能级系统 。

标准隧穿模型将 TLS 态密度描述为在能量上近似常数：单位能量单位体积的 TLS 数量大致与跃迁频率无关。这意味着 TLS 谱密度覆盖整个微波频段。比特工作频率将始终与一群跃迁能量匹配比特能量的 TLS 重叠。

当 TLS 与比特共振耦合时，比特可将其能量转移至 TLS。TLS 随后通过声子发射或其他耗散通道弛豫。比特态丢失。这是能量弛豫通道：它抑制 T1。弛豫速率标度为：

Γ_TLS ∝ n_TLS · |p|²

其中 n_TLS 是比特频率处的 TLS 密度，|p|² 是 TLS-比特耦合的电偶极矩阵元平方，按 TLS 在比特电场体积中的参与比加权。

参与比是关键指标：它量化比特电场能量中有多少驻留在非晶材料中 。将场集中在衬底体材料（低损耗晶态 Si）并最小化与 AlOx 势垒及金属-氧化物界面场重叠的比特设计，将具有更低的参与比，从而更低的 TLS 诱导弛豫速率。这正是钽-硅平台所实现的：Ta 氧化物比 Nb 或 Al 氧化物具有更低的微波损耗，高阻 Si 比蓝宝石具有更低的体损耗 。

但参与比不能被驱动至零。约瑟夫森结需要隧道势垒。势垒是非晶的。比特电场必须与结重叠以实现约瑟夫森非线性。最小参与比受结几何和场分布约束——两者均受比特所需 EJ/EC 比和制造工艺约束。

钽-硅平台是当前材料天花板。它不是 TLS 问题的解决方案。它是 TLS 问题物理边界内的优化。

❌ **工程认知误区：**

“更好的制造”或“更清洁的界面”作用于 TLS 密度前因子，不作用于非晶势垒的基本结构。超高真空沉积减少碳氢化合物污染并提升 T2E 。但 AlOx 势垒仍是非晶的。二能级系统仍然存在。谱密度在能量上仍近似常数。

容错阈值条件要求比特弛豫速率满足 Γ_relax < ε_f / T_gate，其中 ε_f 是目标门错误，T_gate 是门时长。对于 ε_f ~ 10⁻⁴ 和 T_gate ~ 40 ns（普林斯顿平台的最优门时间），所需 Γ_relax < 2.5 × 10³ s⁻¹，对应 T1 > 400 μs。普林斯顿均值 T1 0.45 ms 勉强满足此条件。最高 T1 1.68 ms 有裕度满足。

但 45 比特的均值 T1 为 0.45 ms 。分布有向短寿命的尾部。当比特数扩展至 10³ 及以上时，阵列中至少一个比特落入低 T1 尾部的概率趋近于 1。阵列的纠错性能受限于最差比特，而非最好比特。

✅ **系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）：**

三个杠杆存在，每个都有物理税。

**杠杆 1：通过界面工程降低 TLS 参与比。** 最小化与非晶区的电场重叠。使用 Ta 替代 Nb 用于电容垫（Ta 氧化物损耗更低）。使用高阻 Si 替代蓝宝石（体损耗更低）。使用 UHV 沉积减少结界面碳氢化合物污染 。税：每种材料替代都有其制造约束和复现性挑战。钽-硅平台需要在 Si 上生长 α 相 Ta，施加特定的沉积温度和晶格匹配条件。

· ⚠️ 警告：增大大比特电容垫尺寸增加电容中存储的总电场能量，稀释结势垒的参与比。税：更大垫增加对衬底体损耗和封装级 TLS 的敏感性。参与比是所有材料区域的加权平均。降低结的权重增加衬底的权重。**总 TLS 诱导弛豫速率是所有区域之和。转移权重不消除和。** 执行日期移动数倍，不是数量级。

**为什么不给落地数值？**

· ⚠️ 交付边界：具体的结几何、衬底电阻率、沉积条件和频率分配是你们材料与制造团队在物理红线内的计算责任。本协议交付 TLS 态密度约束（能量上近似常数）、参与比公式、以及容错阈值条件 T1 > ε_f / T_gate。落地参数需要你们的超算在材料栈和几何空间中定位在满足 EJ/EC 比和复现性约束下最小化总加权 TLS 参与比的唯一点。天道定极值，术业有专攻。

📄 **附录：系统级耦合死锁声明**

现代复杂工程是强耦合系统：任何子系统为局部 KPI 改变边界条件，应力与热流沿耦合路径传导至其余 N-1 个子系统。

死锁 04 是死锁 01 相干预算的材料物理基础和放大死锁 03 频率分配问题的良率约束。TLS 诱导 T1 抑制设定比特可用相干时间上限。比特在退相干前可支撑的电路深度受 T1 约束。当阵列扩展至 N = 10³，低 T1 尾部迫使要么比特筛选（良率损失），要么频率失谐至低 TLS 密度区（重新引入死锁 03 的频率碰撞问题）。降低 TLS 密度的材料优化（晶态势垒、UHV 沉积）施加的热预算和制造约束可能与可复现比特频率所需的结均匀性冲突。

**死锁 04 设定相干天花板。死锁 03 消耗裕度。三角闭合。**
