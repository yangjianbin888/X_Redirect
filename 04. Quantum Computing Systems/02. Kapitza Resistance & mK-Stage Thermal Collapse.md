❌ **Core Physical Deadlock:**
Kapitza resistance is not a materials inconvenience. It is the phonon radiation limit at a solid-liquid interface: heat transfer across the boundary is mediated by phonon transmission, and the acoustic impedance mismatch between solid (silver, sapphire, copper) and liquid ³He/⁴He reflects the majority of phonon modes back into the solid . The thermal boundary resistance scales as R_K ∝ T⁻³ below ~100 mK .

At 20 mK, the Kapitza resistance between sintered silver and the ³He-⁴He mixture dominates the entire thermal path. The bulk thermal conductivity of silver is irrelevant. The interface is the bottleneck. Commercial dilution refrigerators achieve 25–30 μW cooling power at 20 mK with optimized sintered silver heat exchangers . This is the physical ceiling of the current architecture, not an engineering shortfall.

Deadlock 01‘s compensation topology demands n_th ≤ 0.01 across N = 10⁴ ions. That requires ≥ 0.2 W of 729 nm laser power delivered to the 4 K stage for parallel sideband cooling. The optical fiber transmission from 300 K to 4 K (approximately 80% through 10 m of fiber) means ≥ 0.2 W of blackbody radiation and scattered light reaches the 4 K plate. Down-converting this load to the 20 mK stage through thermal conduction and radiation yields ≥ 20 mW that must be extracted at the mixing chamber.

**The arithmetic:** 20 mW required extraction ÷ 30 μW available cooling power = **667× deficit**.

❌ **Engineering Misconception:**
“Improve the sinter” or “add more surface area” operates on the Kapitza coefficient, not on its T⁻³ exponent. The specific surface area of sintered silver has a physical ceiling: 50 nm powder at 60% packing ratio, after heat treatment for conductivity, achieves approximately 10 m²/g . Pushing to 20 nm powder increases surface area but degrades thermal conductivity through the sinter, moving the bottleneck from the interface to the bulk. Heat treatment improves conductivity by ~40% but reduces surface area . **Every optimization trades one resistance against another.** The T⁻³ scaling is not negotiable.

✅ **System-Level Compensation Topology (The Only Physical Path):**
The only physically closed-loop path is a two-front assault: **maximize sintered silver surface area to its physical ceiling**, and **reduce the laser heat load at the source**.
