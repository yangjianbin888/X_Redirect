DSP nonlinear equalization converts optical power headroom into digital switching energy, and that energy is removed as die-level heat flux. TCAM power density completes the deadlock: fully associative lookup consumes power linearly with the number of searched prefixes, and at 102.4T line rates, die-level heat flux exceeds package-level cooling removal capacity.
