**❌ Core Physical Deadlock: Backlash as Unobservable State, Compliance as Unmodelable Phase Lag**

A geared actuator has two hidden state variables that the controller cannot directly measure. The first is backlash: the mechanical clearance between gear teeth. When the motor reverses direction, it must rotate through a finite angle—typically 0.05° to 0.5° depending on reducer grade—before the joint output begins to move. During this interval, the motor encoder reports motion. The joint does not move. The controller commands a displacement. The system does not respond. Then, when the backlash is consumed, the joint moves suddenly. The controller sees a step response that does not match its model. It applies correction. The correction overshoots. The system oscillates.

The second hidden state is torsional compliance. A harmonic drive is not rigid. Its flexspline deforms under load. The torsional stiffness is finite—typically 10³ to 10⁴ Nm/rad. When the motor applies torque, the joint output lags in phase. The motor encoder sees the motor’s position. The joint output is somewhere else. The controller assumes the motor position is the joint position. It is wrong.

These are not noise. They are deterministic state errors. The controller cannot model what it cannot measure. When the closed-loop gain is tuned for precision, it amplifies the very error it cannot observe. The result is a limit cycle: the controller commands, the joint responds late, the controller corrects, the joint overshoots. The robot does not execute precision tasks. It oscillates.

**❌ Engineering Misconception: “Higher Encoder Resolution Solves It”**

The industry response has been to increase encoder resolution. This measures the motor more precisely. It does not measure the joint output more precisely. A 20-bit encoder on the motor shaft reports motor position with arcsecond resolution. It does not report backlash. It does not report torsional deflection. The joint output remains unobserved. Higher motor encoder resolution makes the controller more confident in a model that is structurally wrong. The oscillation persists.

**✅ System-Level Compensation Topology (Only Exit)**

The only path that does not involve pretending the hidden state does not exist is to measure it or to design it out. The absolute extreme parameters derived from first principles:

- **Maximum backlash before precision manipulation becomes physically impossible: <0.01° per joint.** Beyond this, the dead zone width exceeds the positional tolerance for insertion and assembly tasks.
- **Minimum torsional stiffness to push the compliance-induced phase lag above the control bandwidth: >10⁵ Nm/rad.** Beyond this, the phase lag at the control crossover frequency is small enough that a passivity-based controller can shape the loop without instability.
- **Minimum joint encoder resolution to observe backlash directly: ≤1 arcsecond.** Below this, the dead zone is sub-resolution and cannot be measured in real time.

**❌ 核心物理死锁：背隙作为不可观测状态，柔性作为不可建模相位滞后**

一个齿轮传动执行器有两个控制器无法直接测量的隐藏状态变量。第一个是背隙：齿轮齿之间的机械间隙。当电机反转方向时，它必须旋转一个有限角度——通常为0.05°至0.5°，取决于减速器等级——关节输出才开始移动。在这个区间内，电机编码器报告运动。关节不动。控制器命令位移。系统不响应。然后，当背隙被消耗完毕，关节突然移动。控制器看到一个与其模型不匹配的阶跃响应。它施加修正。修正过冲。系统震荡。

第二个隐藏状态是扭转柔性。谐波减速器不是刚性的。它的柔轮在负载下变形。扭转刚度是有限的——通常为10³至10⁴ Nm/rad。当电机施加扭矩时，关节输出相位滞后。电机编码器看到电机的位置。关节输出在别处。控制器假设电机位置就是关节位置。它错了。

这些不是噪声。它们是确定性的状态误差。控制器无法建模它无法测量的东西。当闭环增益为精密而调谐时，它放大了它无法观测的那个误差。结果是极限环：控制器命令，关节迟响应，控制器修正，关节过冲。机器人不执行精密任务。它震荡。

**❌ 工程认知误区：“更高编码器分辨率就能解决”**

产业的回应是提高编码器分辨率。这更精确地测量电机。它没有更精确地测量关节输出。一个20位编码器在电机轴上以角秒分辨率报告电机位置。它不报告背隙。它不报告扭转形变。关节输出仍然未被观测。更高的电机编码器分辨率让控制器对一个结构上错误的模型更加自信。震荡持续。

**✅ 系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）**
