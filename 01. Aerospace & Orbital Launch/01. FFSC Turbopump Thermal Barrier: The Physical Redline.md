### ❌ Core Physical Deadlock

The oxidizer-rich preburner operates at a PO2 where the Gibbs free energy of formation for NiO is negative. The reaction Ni(s) + ½ O₂(g) → NiO(s) proceeds spontaneously. The oxidation rate follows Arrhenius kinetics: k = A·exp(-Ea/RT). At Raptor 3's 35 MPa chamber pressure, the preburner oxygen partial pressure is higher than any previous FFSC engine in serial production. The driving force for nickel oxidation is not a design variable. It is a thermodynamic boundary condition.

The oxidation rate constant at the preburner wall temperature determines wall recession per cycle. For Ni-base superalloys in high-pressure oxygen at 900–1100 K, the parabolic rate constant kp for NiO growth is on the order of **10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹**. At Raptor 3's mass flow of 800 kg/s and preburner residence time, the integrated oxidation damage per hot-fire cycle produces measurable wall thickness loss. This is not a coating problem. This is a thermodynamic death sentence with a variable execution date.

### ❌ Engineering Misconception

The reflex is to coat the preburner wall. Aluminide diffusion coatings, MCrAlY overlays, ceramic barrier layers. Each fails at the ignition transient.

Any barrier layer with sufficient thermodynamic stability to resist oxygen attack at 35 MPa has a thermal expansion coefficient divergent from the Ni-alloy substrate. At ignition, wall temperature sweeps from cryogenic to operational in under one second. The interfacial strain Δε = Δα·ΔT exceeds the fracture strain of the coating. The barrier cracks. Oxygen finds alloy. The clock resets.

The second reflex is to purge or dilute the preburner oxygen. This destroys the FFSC cycle efficiency. The oxidizer-rich preburner exists precisely to drive the oxygen turbine with oxygen-rich gas. Diluting it with inert species reduces turbine work, reduces pump output, reduces chamber pressure. The architecture is self-contradictory.

The third reflex is software. No control algorithm changes the sign of a Gibbs free energy. No sensor suite slows an Arrhenius rate constant. The engine controller can trim mixture ratio, throttle thrust, adjust igniter timing. It cannot alter the thermodynamic driving force for nickel oxidation at the preburner wall.

### ✅ System-Level Compensation Topology (Only Exit)

**Path A: Alumina-forming Ni-superalloy with graded transition layer.**

An alloy that forms a continuous α-Al₂O₃ scale at the preburner surface offers a kinetic barrier orders of magnitude more effective than NiO or Cr₂O₃. The parabolic rate constant for α-Al₂O₃ growth at 900–1100 K in high-PO₂ environments is **10⁻¹² to 10⁻¹¹ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹**, compared to **10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹** for NiO. At equivalent wall temperature, oxidation life extends by a factor of **10² to 10³**.

The physical cost: alumina-forming alloys require Al content **≥ 4–6 wt%** to sustain scale rehealing after thermal cycling. This reduces γ′ precipitate volume fraction, lowering yield strength at 700–900 K by **15–25%** versus baseline Ni-base superalloys. The preburner wall must thicken. Wall thickening reduces regenerative cooling channel cross-section. Cooling efficiency drops. Thermal margin narrows.

**Path B: Transient thermal shaping with transpiration cooling.**

A **50 K reduction** in peak preburner wall temperature reduces the oxidation rate constant by a factor of **2.5 to 4**, depending on effective activation energy. Transpiration cooling with **3–8%** of preburner oxygen flow bled through porous wall segments achieves this reduction during the steady-state burn. Transient throttling during the first **10–15 seconds** keeps wall temperature below the oxidation knee until the protective scale establishes steady-state thickness.

The physical cost: transpiration cooling reduces specific impulse by **1.5–3 seconds** due to coolant mass not participating in main combustion. Transient throttling extends time-to-full-thrust by **12–18 seconds**, a mission-profile constraint for boostback and landing burns.

### ❌ 核心物理死锁

富氧预燃室运行在 NiO 生成吉布斯自由能为负的氧分压区间。反应 Ni(s) + ½ O₂(g) → NiO(s) 自发进行。氧化速率遵循阿伦尼乌斯动力学：k = A·exp(-Ea/RT)。在 Raptor 3 的 35 MPa 室压下，预燃室氧分压高于此前任何进入批产的全流量分级燃烧发动机 。镍氧化的驱动力不是设计变量。它是热力学边界条件。

预燃室壁温下的氧化速率常数决定每循环壁面退让量。镍基高温合金在 900–1100 K 高压氧气中的 NiO 生长抛物线速率常数 kp 约为 **10⁻⁹ 至 10⁻⁸ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹** 。在 Raptor 3 的 800 kg/s 质量流量与预燃室停留时间下，每次热试车循环的积分氧化损伤产生可测量的壁厚损失。这不是涂层问题。这是一份热力学死刑判决书，执行日期可调。

### ❌ 工程认知误区

本能反应是给预燃室壁加涂层。铝化物扩散涂层、MCrAlY 覆盖层、陶瓷屏障层。每一种都死于点火瞬态。

任何具备足够热力学稳定性以在 35 MPa 下抵抗氧攻击的屏障层，其热膨胀系数都与镍基合金基体分道扬镳。点火时，壁温在不到一秒内从低温扫至工作温度。界面应变 Δε = Δα·ΔT 超过涂层的断裂应变。屏障开裂。氧气找到合金。时钟重置。

第二个本能反应是吹除或稀释预燃室氧气。这摧毁全流量分级燃烧循环效率。富氧预燃室存在的全部意义就是用富氧燃气驱动氧涡轮。用惰性组分稀释它，涡轮功下降，泵输出下降，室压下降。这个架构是自相矛盾的。

第三个本能反应是软件。没有任何控制算法能改变吉布斯自由能的符号。没有任何传感器套件能减慢阿伦尼乌斯速率常数。发动机控制器可以修剪混合比、节流推力、调整点火时序。它不能改变预燃室壁面镍氧化的热力学驱动力。

### ✅ 系统级代偿拓扑（唯一生路）

**路径 A：氧化铝形成型镍基高温合金 + 梯度过渡层。**

在预燃室表面形成连续 α-Al₂O₃ 氧化皮的合金，提供的动力学屏障比 NiO 或 Cr₂O₃ 有效数个数量级。α-Al₂O₃ 在 900–1100 K 高氧分压环境下的抛物线速率常数约为 **10⁻¹² 至 10⁻¹¹ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹**，而 NiO 为 **10⁻⁹ 至 10⁻⁸ g²·cm⁻⁴·s⁻¹**。在同等壁温下，氧化寿命延长 **10² 至 10³ 倍**。

物理代价：氧化铝形成型合金需要 Al 含量 **≥ 4–6 wt%** 以在热循环后维持氧化皮再愈合能力。这降低 γ′ 析出相体积分数，使 700–900 K 下的屈服强度比基线镍基高温合金下降 **15–25%**。预燃室壁必须增厚。壁厚增加压缩再生冷却通道截面。冷却效率下降。热裕度收窄。

**路径 B：瞬态热整形 + 发汗冷却。**

峰值预燃室壁温降低 **50 K**，氧化速率常数下降 **2.5 至 4 倍**，取决于有效活化能。通过预燃室氧气流量 **3–8%** 的发汗冷却，在稳态燃烧段实现该降温。运行前 **10–15 秒**的瞬态节流将壁温压在氧化拐点以下，直到保护性氧化皮建立稳态厚度。

物理代价：发汗冷却因冷却剂质量不参与主燃烧而降低比冲 **1.5–3 秒**。瞬态节流将满推力到达时间延长 **12–18 秒**，对回收助推和着陆燃烧构成任务剖面约束。
