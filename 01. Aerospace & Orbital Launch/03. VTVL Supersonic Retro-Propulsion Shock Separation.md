### ❌ Core Physical Deadlock

During supersonic retro-propulsion, the exhaust plume exits the nozzle into a freestream whose static pressure exceeds the nozzle exit pressure. The plume is overexpanded. The counter-flowing freestream and the plume meet at a contact surface. A bow shock forms upstream of this contact surface, decelerating the freestream to subsonic conditions . The stand-off distance of this bow shock from the vehicle base is not a fixed geometric property. It is a dynamic variable coupled to the plume pressure field.

DLR URANS simulations of engine startup during retro-propulsion show that ignition at high altitude initially causes the exhaust plume to penetrate the bow shock before pushing it away from the vehicle . The transient evolution of the plume significantly alters the aerodynamic characteristics during startup. This is the first unsteady mechanism: shock stand-off oscillation driven by plume establishment and chamber pressure fluctuation.

The second mechanism is more severe. When the vehicle flies at an angle of attack during retro-propulsion, the oncoming airflow deflects the exhaust gas toward the leeward side. At high angles of attack, the windward side of the aft cylindrical section is exposed to almost uncontaminated freestream air. This introduces a normal force at a location far from the center of gravity, generating a strong pitching moment . The separation shock on the windward side migrates upstream as the boundary layer thickens. The migration speed and final equilibrium position are sensitive to boundary layer thickness, which increases with altitude, wall temperature, or surface gas injection .

The control surface actuators and engine gimbal must counter this pitching moment within the available torque budget. The separation shock traversal time scale is set by the boundary layer response and the plume pressure coupling, not by the actuator bandwidth.

### ❌ Engineering Misconception

The first reflex is to increase thrust vector control authority. Larger gimbal angles, faster actuators, higher torque budgets. This addresses the symptom, not the coupling. The separation shock position is a function of thrust coefficient CT. Increasing thrust to gain control authority increases CT, which drives the separation shock further upstream, which increases the side load the control system must counter. The control authority and the disturbance scale together. The margin does not close.

The second reflex is to rely on base region shielding. The plume does shield the baseplate from direct freestream impingement, and drag reduction has been observed to be an order of magnitude lower than plume-off conditions, with negative drag coefficients reported in some configurations . But shielding the base does not shield the cylindrical section forward of the separation point. When the separation shock migrates upstream past the base region, the cylindrical section experiences the full dynamic pressure of the post-shock flow. The shielding argument fails precisely when the separation shock has moved furthest upstream.

The third reflex is to treat the landing burn as a separate, benign regime. The landing burn occurs at transonic or subsonic conditions where no bow shock is present between the exhaust and the freestream . But the transition from supersonic retro-propulsion to subsonic landing burn passes through a transonic corridor where the shock structure collapses and the plume transitions from blunt mode to long penetration mode . The unsteady loads during this transition are not captured by steady-state analysis of either endpoint.

### ❌ 核心物理死锁

超音速反推过程中，排气羽流从喷管喷入静压高于喷管出口压力的自由来流。羽流处于过膨胀状态。反向自由来流与羽流在接触面相遇。弓形激波在接触面上游形成，将自由来流减速至亚音速 。该弓形激波距飞行器底部的驻定距离不是固定的几何属性。它是与羽流压力场耦合的动态变量。

DLR 对反推过程中发动机启动的 URANS 模拟表明，高空点火最初导致排气羽流穿透弓形激波，随后将激波推离飞行器 。羽流的瞬态演化在启动过程中显著改变气动特性。这是第一个非定常机制：激波驻定距离随羽流建立与燃烧室压力脉动而振荡。

第二个机制更为严峻。当飞行器在反推过程中以攻角飞行时，迎面气流将排气偏转向背风侧。在大攻角下，尾部圆柱段的迎风侧暴露于几乎未受污染的来流中。这在远离质心的位置引入法向力，产生强烈的俯仰力矩 。迎风侧的分离激波随边界层增厚而向上游迁移。迁移速度与最终平衡位置对边界层厚度敏感，而边界层厚度随高度、壁温或表面气体引射而增加 。

控制面作动器与发动机万向节必须在可用扭矩预算内对抗该俯仰力矩。分离激波迁移的时间尺度由边界层响应与羽流压力耦合设定，而非作动器带宽。

### ❌ 工程认知误区

第一个本能反应是增加推力矢量控制权限。更大的万向节角度、更快的作动器、更高的扭矩预算。这处理的是症状，不是耦合。分离激波位置是推力系数 CT 的函数。增加推力以获得控制权限会增大 CT，驱动分离激波进一步上游，增大控制系统必须对抗的侧载。控制权限与扰动尺度同步增长。裕度不闭合。

第二个本能反应是依赖底部区域屏蔽。羽流确实屏蔽底部免受自由来流直接撞击，阻力降低量级可达羽流关闭工况的十分之一，部分构型甚至报告负阻力系数 。但屏蔽底部不等于屏蔽分离点前方的圆柱段。当分离激波迁移至底部区域上游时，圆柱段承受激波后流动的全部动压。屏蔽论证恰好在分离激波迁移最远时失效。

第三个本能反应是将着陆点火视为独立的良性 regime。着陆点火发生在跨音速或亚音速条件，排气与自由来流之间不存在弓形激波 。但从超音速反推到亚音速着陆的过渡穿越一个跨音速走廊，激波结构在此崩溃，羽流从钝头模式向长穿透模式转换 。过渡期间的非定常载荷不被任一端点的稳态分析捕获。

超音速反推是强耦合系统。分离激波位置响应羽流压力与边界层厚度。边界层厚度响应壁温与表面引射。壁温响应激波后流动的气动加热。控制系统响应侧载，侧载响应激波位置。任何单点修改沿此回路传播。增加推力以获得控制权限，CT 上升，分离激波上游迁移，侧载上升，万向节权限被它本应对抗的扰动消耗。局部控制权限优化是全球姿态发散的种子。单点突破是物理死路。唯一物理出路：放弃单点思维，从激波-边界层-羽流-控制全耦合视角重构拓扑。这是“霸王协议”交付的核心——一张全局分离激波轨迹相图。你们可以自己解，代价是制导导航控制、推进、气动团队在物理死锁中互相抵消所损失的时间，加上 **12–18 个月**的不可逆表征滞后。
